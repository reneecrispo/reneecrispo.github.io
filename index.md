# About me

I am a 1st year PhD student at [INRIA, Paris](https://www.inria.fr/fr) and Sorbonne Université, working within the [COMMEDIA project team](https://team.inria.fr/commedia/) under the supervision of [Miguel A. Fernàndez](https://team.inria.fr/commedia/fernandez/). My research focuses on computational modeling of cardiac hemodynamics in Hypoplastic Left Heart Syndrome (HLHS), with the goal of better understanding patient-specific blood and oxygen flow and supporting the evaluation of surgical strategies.

In my work, I use a kinematically uncoupled framework for 3D cardiac simulations, where myocardial motion is prescribed independently of the fluid solver to improve efficiency. The model combines incompressible Navier–Stokes equations in moving domains, reduced-order valve representations, and closed-loop circulation models, and oxygen transport model.
