# About me

I am a 1st year PhD student at [INRIA, Paris](https://www.inria.fr/fr) and a member of the [Laboratoire Jacques-Louis-Lions](https://www.ljll.fr) at Sorbonne Universite. I work within the [COMMEDIA project team](https://team.inria.fr/commedia/), under the supervision of [Miguel A. Fernàndez](https://team.inria.fr/commedia/fernandez/), on modeling and simulation of cardiac hemodynamics in Hypoplastic Left Heart Syndrome (HLHS).

My research combines applied mathematics and scientific computing to build reliable and clinically meaningful cardiac models.
